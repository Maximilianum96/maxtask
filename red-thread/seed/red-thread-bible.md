# Red Thread (Der rote Faden): design and story bible

v0.2, August 2026. Living document. LOCKED = canon. DRAFT = proposed, awaiting yes. GAP = needs Max or Mama.

## 0. How to use this

This is the game's single source of truth. When a design question comes up, this document answers it or gets updated. Fill gaps in any order, as text or voice memos. Nothing here is final except what is marked LOCKED, and even that bends if the gut says so. It is your father, your game.

## 1. Vision

**Logline (LOCKED).** An isometric action RPG about Aristoteles, son of the weaver whose thread the Fates cut too early. As the world he quietly held together unravels, Ari travels his father's life backwards to demand him back, and learns instead to weave him in. Nur so gehts.

**Pillars (LOCKED).**
1. One thread told, the whole family visible.
2. Neither Reed state is the good world.
3. Show, never say.
4. The father is scarce. Scarcity is presence.
5. Better, not more: one strong set per region, 2 to 4 hours of play, small and true.
6. A child's memory rebuilt with a man's craft.
7. Animals are never enemies. Nothing in this world cuts except the Fates' shears. Enemies only fray, snag and unravel.
8. Round is alive, straight is wrong: healthy woven things live in curves and loops, the unraveling pulls things straight, taut and pointed.

**The Kharg lens (LOCKED).** From Arc the Lad: Twilight of the Spirits, the childhood favorite, played from the human brother's side. The opening stays domestic: myth arrives into the ordinary, never the reverse. The weave world of Spinners and frayed things stays other and wondrous, always seen from the human side with a child's eyes; Ari is a visitor to myth, never a native. And Ari spends the game fighting the very acceptance his father's phrase teaches; the false ending is where that wrongness finally breaks open.

**GAP, the promise to yourself.** One paragraph in Max's own words: why this game. To reread on hard days. Nobody else ever needs to see it.

## 2. Story

### 2.1 The myth

LOCKED: The Fates spin, measure and cut every thread. The father was a weaver who tied threads between distant places; since his thread was cut, the world frays. Holes in the sky, people fraying at the edges, passages unraveling. The Spinners keep their seat at the Institut above St. Gallen. The law of cloth: it only holds at the right tension, too slack fails, too tight fails.

GAP: What the Fates are like when finally met. Three sisters, a single voice, a vast indifferent loom, a bureaucracy of thread?

### 2.2 Act structure

**Prologue, Der See.** Four Zürich homes around the lake as playable vignettes, each teaching one mechanic: the city (two threads newly tied), Zollikon (first patterns), Zollikerberg (the view widens), Horgen (the family cloth, the warmest room in the game). Ends in present day Horgen: the unraveling begins at home. Ari finds the Reed and the loom in the attic; the morning ritual is learned here.
DRAFT, first sixty seconds: a cat bats a loose red thread; the camera follows the thread across the floor, up the stairs, into the attic, to the loom. Title card.
DRAFT, inciting signs: the animals feel it first. Cows will not graze, horses stand staring at the lake. Wrongness before spectacle, felt by creatures who never lie.
DRAFT: the first vision arrives the first morning Ari strings the Reed. The father's tool, used the father's way, calls him.

**The journey, his life walked backwards (LOCKED in principle).** Zürich, then the stations in reverse: London, USA (postcard), Munich, St. Gallen, then south into the light, Málaga, Mallorca. Chile's placement pending Mama's timeline.

**Act one climax (LOCKED).** Ari dies to the first boss, Das Nadelkissen, losing to sheer weight, not trickery. A mittelgroße hand catches his. One frame of the grin. "Nur so gehts!" Never explained, never repeated.

**Midpoint (LOCKED).** The sampler in the Horgen attic workshop: Nur so gehts, stitched. Later a letter or neighbor reveals he stitched it after a failure, through his teeth.

**Act three.** At the Spinners' seat Ari wins the right to have the thread rewoven. The false ending is playable: the world with him back, subtly wrong, everything "fine". The tell (LOCKED): the rewoven father never turns away when moved. The shoulders never move once. The player who learned his tell feels the wrongness before naming it. DRAFT: Ari must unravel the false world himself, by hand, the hardest input in the game. In the final act the visions stop entirely; the last adult vision before the silence is the Umarmung, the goodbye he always gave.

**True ending (LOCKED).** On the Mallorca shore where his thread was first spun (born 12 July 1963, Palma), Ari weaves the cut thread into his own pattern. The world holds because something new holds. His posture and his father's walk are now the same animation. The final vision is a boy on the shore, waving once.

**Post credits (LOCKED).** Ari appears as the vision to someone else, calls them by a name only he would know, speaks only the phrase. In that stranger's home live two young cats, one of them carrying an old cat's name.

### 2.3 Writing rules

LOCKED: "Nur so gehts" exactly three times, always German in every language version. The cause of the cut is never explained; current canon says only: the thread was cut. That door is Max's alone, no deadline, no wrong answer. Humor is allowed and wanted, he was schallendes Lachen. The mother's hearts appear only in her handwriting. Visions stay in a child's colors, mythic, warm, slightly larger than life.

### 2.4 Inspiration on file

Arc the Lad: Twilight of the Spirits (EU title: Arc: Twilight of the Spirits). Steals adopted: the heirloom that summons (stone halves awaken the spirit, Reed summons the vision); dual campaign convergence as ancestor of the series as cloth idea; circular positioning translated into thread geometry; consumable Spirit Stones validating the tension fibre economy; the Big Owl honored as Ari's owl. OST is reference listening for MAR.

## 3. Characters

### Aristoteles, "Ari"

LOCKED: Everyone calls him Ari or the weaver's boy; only the visions use the full name. His posture straightens across the game until his walk matches his father's. Skips breakfast, goes quietly hangry. Fails a weave three times, the screen tightens. Inherits the stubbornness honestly.
Child Ari in Der See (LOCKED, reference RT-011): the curly mop, hands always one notch more detailed than the rest of him, runs with airplane arms through safe meadows. Warm beat: on an owl flight between stations a kindly stranger mistakes the long curled child for a girl; he corrects them with a grin.
GAP: Age at start. Does he paint? Voice or quiet protagonist? Three traits of Max's he carries, three deliberately his own.

### Der Weber, the father

LOCKED: Booming laugh (the secret found sound). Confident upright gait (how a faceless figure is recognized). Mittelgroße hands (the catch is an ordinary hand). Approached strangers first. Cried at romance and at births; turned away when moved, shoulders once, then back with the grin. Proud and not always able to say it. Ambitious, hard on himself in London. Sociable, then needed quiet at home. 7:30, wash, shared breakfast, loving goodbye with a hug. Steak, Italian and Spanish food, her cooking. Read all newspapers and books. Music: blues, soul, Stones, Prince, Sinatra, Etta James, Falco, Salomon Burke, Klassik. Vision arc: acts, then advises, then appears, then absence.
The vision's gestures (LOCKED, reference RT-015): exactly two. The catch when Ari falls, and the silent applause when Ari does something worthy. The vision is the father with the pride finally visible.
The medals (LOCKED, reference RT-015): woven honors, rosettes of ribbon and thread on his chest, the marks nations gave the man who tied them together. They regain their color one by one as Ari collects the unsaid pride sentences; by the final act the chest is full.
GAP: What he feared. What he hummed. One story from Chile. His dream at 30.

### Die Mutter

LOCKED: Keeper of the timeline. Her letters waymark every station, the only handwriting with hearts in the margins. Her cooking at the Horgen table is the deepest healing in the game. She said no to the name Aristoteles.
DRAFT: She sends Ari off, and the letters are how she travels with him.
GAP: The no to the name story as a warm in game joke? Door open: her real voice reading the letters, only if she ever offers.

### Cindy, the companion

LOCKED: Ari's travel cat, named for the real cat of the years 8 to 18. She is the herald of the visions: ears turn first, tail goes still, eyes fixed on empty air half a second before he arrives. No UI, no chime, just the cat. In the final act the visions stop and Cindy stops staring, and the player feels the absence through the animal. On the Mallorca shore she stares one last long time at the water, at the boy, then turns and looks at Ari. End.
The cats of the world: the Horgen hub house keeps three cats, one of them called Zoey.
GAP (RT-016, open commission): what the real Cindy looked like, markings, colors, how she sat.

### The sisters

LOCKED: Present, not followed. At the Horgen table, in the family cloth Ari reweaves, in photographs. Their threads are their own colors, reserved. Series north star: each game one thread, the series is the cloth. His is red, Mama's gold. Any future thread is decided together with the family, on solid ground, when its time comes.
GAP: Their colors and fibres. How present in Horgen scenes.

### Supporting cast

LOCKED: Reed users recognizable by the comb at the belt, a quiet nod. Strangers across the stations each hold one pride sentence. Apothecaries and haberdashers sell tension fibre, paid in cloth.
GAP: Two or three recurring road faces, if any.

## 4. Stations

- **Der See / Zürich (full chapter, prologue).** Fibre: linen. Four homes, one lake. LOCKED.
- **Horgen (hub).** The house reweaves room by room. Every seventh day: Brotzeit, sauna, the radio Krimi with collectible episodes. Three cats. LOCKED.
- **London (full chapter).** The great exchange where people trade away years of their own thread. His thread pulled too tight here: ambition, pressure, missing her. The young man's love letters are found in this act. Fibre: wool, confirmed by the loop wool sheep.
- **USA / Whittier (postcard).** Bright, glossy, synthetic thread that cannot hold a pattern. The single memory: him writing home, missing her. Tender, not mocking. LOCKED as postcard.
- **Munich (full chapter, LOCKED).** The only station with two sets of footprints: the father at seventeen falling in love on these streets, and the boy who came at eight without him, the Gräfelfing decade, ages 8 to 18, the Cindy years. Recognition moments cluster here; Ari's own memories begin bleeding into the father's stations. Includes the Gräfelfing meadow vignette: barefoot summer grass, the first Nadel sting, where it really happened. GAP: one more specific Munich scene.
- **St. Gallen (chapter, compact).** The Institut on its hill, seat of the Spinners. Act three begins. Fibre: nettle, high in the Alps.
- **Chile (full chapter, pending).** The ambassador years. Fibre: alpaca; the Atacama as the grey slack thread desert. GAP: everything, awaiting Mama's timeline and one story.
- **Málaga (chapter).** The schoolyard by the sea, flamenco guitar over soul. Fibre: silk.
- **Mallorca (finale).** The shore, July 1963. The true ending.

Production rule LOCKED: four or five full chapters maximum, everything else a postcard. Updated DRAFT picks: Der See, London, Munich, Chile, and the Málaga plus Mallorca finale arc as one; St. Gallen compact.

### 4.5 The friendly world (LOCKED, references RT-001 to RT-010)

Patched cows: cloth patches, animals lovingly mended by their farmers, a quietly repaired world. Loop wool sheep: wool drawn as loops of thread, soft walking tangles, animals made of the world's material. A village dog in little knitted wool boots. The Igel as needle gatherer: trundles the meadows collecting lost pins, the living pincushion, the tailor's oldest friend; the path behind an Igel is safe. The Eichhörnchen as bobbin hoarder: caches spools in tree hollows, befriend them to find stashes; tail is two thirds of the silhouette. The bee, innocent always: flax pollinator, wax strengthens thread at the bench. The swan on Der See; DRAFT: the first hole in the sky appears as its broken reflection beside a swan before the player ever looks up. Sparrows on the rooftops. The Owl: Ari's travel guide who flies the passport map between stations (the Big Owl honored), and perches in the Horgen attic above the loom, the night reader's bird.

## 5. Systems

### 5.1 Fabric economy (LOCKED)

No gold. Enemies drop what they were made of: thread, scraps, cloth. Regional fibres as above. All gear is clothing Ari weaves, visible on his body, the red thread through the best pieces. One strong set per region. Late game: weave for NPCs, repair frayed people themselves.

### 5.2 The Reed (LOCKED rules)

His father's weaver's comb, strung with tension fibre that wears out. Chosen each morning, never mid fight. Gathered wild or bought prepared. Small carry limit, so the question is planning, not rationing. Running out is story. Never a moral meter, never tied to the weaver's fate.
Unstrung: many threads at once, shimmer, glowing side paths, associative leaps, wandering main thread, hyperfocus that greys the world around one brilliant thing.
Strung: aligned threads, steady aim, clean weaving, constant main thread, dimmed shimmer, doors that never appear.
GAP, most important: both states from inside. Sound, color, how time moves, what a room feels like. Raw voice memo is perfect.
GAP: the morning stringing as a thirty second scene.

### 5.3 Visions (LOCKED)

Faceless red thread figure at first, recognized by gait. The eyes resolve first, wet, at a moment of pride. Face completes as letters, stories and recognition moments accumulate. Frequency falls act by act until the final silence.

### 5.4 Recognition moments

LOCKED concept: some visions unlock through how you play, not what you find. The world recognizes him in Ari before Ari does.
GAP: Max's list, five to ten entries, where he already recognizes the father in himself.

### 5.5 The unsaid pride (LOCKED)

Strangers hold the sentences he said about his family when they were not in the room. Ari collects what the child never heard. Each collected sentence returns color to one medal on the vision's chest.
Note: seven real sentences exist, in her voice. Whether they enter verbatim, echoed, or stay private is a decision with no deadline. Reserved if ever wanted: the dedication. Für einen grossen Mann.

### 5.6 Death and the thread

GAP, big open design question: what does dying cost? Options: the cloak frays and must be rewoven (cost: fabric). Someone unseen reweaves you, you wake at the last camp, one thread paler. A Fate tallies every reweave and mentions it once, late.

### 5.7 Combat and skill trees

DRAFT: weapons are the weaver's tools, shuttle, needle, distaff, never shears (only the Fates hold shears). Four fibre trees: linen (light and memory), hemp (resilience), silk (speed and deception), nettle (pain turned to armor, unlocked late). Signature battlefield verb: stretch a thread between two anchor points; everything crossing the line is snared or frayed apart. Thread geometry instead of grid positioning.
GAP: Ari's starting tree, three ability fantasies per tree, in Max's words. Titan Quest instincts welcome.

### 5.8 Daily ritual (LOCKED)

7:30, wash, shared breakfast as the day's buff, string the Reed or leave it, a farewell. Sundays in Horgen slow everything down.

### 5.9 The Frayed (enemy design, references RT-012 to RT-014)

Rules (LOCKED): enemies are textile horrors, never animals. They fray, snag and unravel; they never cut. Shears exist in exactly one pair of hands in this universe, the Fates'.
The fear law (LOCKED): the game only needs to hurt you once, honestly, and the player's attention does the rest forever. One real sting buys tension in every soft meadow, no respawning swarms required.
**Zwirnling (RT-012), basic enemy.** A figure wound of thread around a bobbin body, carrying a lost pin as a spear, with a stitched on smile: embroidered, pleasant, unchanging.
**Nadeln, hazard.** Lost pins and needles hiding in soft meadow grass and cloth terrain, invisible until stepped on, one sharp sting far out of proportion to their size. The first one gets Ari in the Gräfelfing meadow, barefoot.
**Das Nadelkissen (RT-013), Act One boss.** The Igel's dark mirror, the Great Pincushion, hoarder of stolen pins. Slow but punishing, spiked so careless jumps hurt, mace tail, huge health, low cunning. Its one enormous hit is the scripted death that triggers the catch.
**The Triplets (RT-014), boss guarding the Spinners' seat.** Three identical figures cut from the same Schnittmuster, paper pattern people, tissue thin, vanishing when they turn edge on. Low health, lots of tricks, annoying on purpose, and the double and swap lasts only shortly: a brief shuffle flurry, then a calm honest phase. Trick, breathe, trick, breathe. Only the true one's defeat ends it. Trains the exact recognition skill the false ending will need. Echo of the three Fates.

## 6. Art direction

LOCKED: hand painted, released under MAR. AI in the kitchen, never on the plate: AI may propose variations and specs, only MAR paints; no AI generated final art anywhere in the shipped game.
LOCKED, the line law (references RT-001 to RT-015): round is alive, straight is wrong. Healthy woven things live in curves and loops; the unraveling is straight, taut, pointed. In the memory world, everything soft (wool, curls, clouds) is loops of the same line. Naive child hand renderings inside visions and memories are intentional meaning, not compromise.
DRAFT: the father's thread is the only pure red in the entire game.
GAP: palette words per region. Reference board. The single test screen: the lake, one scribble sheep, Cindy on the path, painted once at real size.
